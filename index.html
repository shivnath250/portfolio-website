The story so far

  1. 2017–2023 · Pune

    An engineer’s grounding

    B.Tech, Mechanical · Vishwakarma Institute of Technology

    Mechanical engineering at Vishwakarma Institute of Technology, graduating at 9.37/10 — a hands-on designer (SolidWorks CSWA-certified) with a run of final-year builds. One of them, a smart pesticide-sprayer robot that takes the manual labour and the fumes out of orchard spraying, earned a granted Indian design patent. Captained the department kabaddi team, too — a first lesson in leading from the front.

  2. 2022 · Pune

    Learning to see waste

    Operations Intern · GE Aerospace

    A production-floor internship at GE Aerospace, where Lean Six Sigma, 5S, and value-stream mapping stopped being textbook terms. In six months I helped cut work-in-progress inventory by 25% and lift throughput by 30%, and aligned upstream and downstream cells to remove flow interruptions at both handoffs.

  3. 2023–2026 · Udupi

    Making the business case

    Assistant Manager · Adani Power

    Assistant Manager on a 2×600 MW subcritical thermal plant — performance engineering across heat rate, auxiliary power, and boiler and turbine efficiency. Three initiatives ran on one pattern: read the operating data, build the case, win management approval, and measure the result.

    • Built the case for a single-unit cooling strategy and secured approval, improving efficiency and reducing fuel consumption — ~₹30 lakh in annual fuel savings.
    • Coordinated operations, maintenance, and instrumentation on a 12-unit equipment optimization — ~₹38 lakh in annual savings.
    • Automated data capture into an equipment-level power dashboard, cutting report-prep time by 80% and freeing analyst time for root-cause work.

  4. 2026 · Ahmedabad

    The wider view

    Assistant Manager · Adani Power

    Moved to headquarters and owned risk escalation across a PAN-India thermal portfolio — driving three major interventions to closure (₹1.2 crore saved), and rebuilding leadership reporting end-to-end to free ~0.25 FTE (468 hours a year) for higher-value analysis.

  5. 2026–2027 · Chennai

    Sharpening the lens

    PGPM Candidate · Great Lakes Institute of Management

    A one-year PGPM at Great Lakes Institute of Management, where I hold a 3.71/4 GPA with a dual specialization in Consulting and Operations. It’s a case-method, lab-driven program built on structured problem-solving and data-driven decision-making — sharpening an operator’s instinct into a manager’s toolkit. Beyond class I sit on the Consulting Club core committee, take the floor in case debates, and stay competitive on the sports field. As Project Lead for Karma Yoga, I ran a field study with the Noble Nayakas in a Tamil Nadu farming village — a business plan around soil health, drip irrigation, and solar power for climate resilience.
